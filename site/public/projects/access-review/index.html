Microsoft 365 access review

A review queue for account and license findings, using deterministic synthetic fixtures. The PowerShell evaluator is runnable locally; this page shows a fixed sample report.

THE PROBLEM

Turn account evidence into a reviewable queue.

A disabled account can retain a license. Missing sign-in evidence is not proof of inactivity. The evaluator reports what needs investigation and leaves every account unchanged.

Repeatable inputs

An explicit report date and fixture file make results reproducible. The evaluator rejects duplicate IDs, non-boolean account status, invalid thresholds and sign-in dates after the report date.

Human decision

Every row says “Report only.” There are no write calls, automatic license removals or tenant credentials. Findings are prompts for review rather than instructions to revoke access.
